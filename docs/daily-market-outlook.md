# Daily market outlook

The Persian **چشم‌انداز روزانه** dashboard tab explains conditional 3–7 day scenarios. It is independent of hourly entry signals and never creates orders or proposed setups. It is not a months-long investment forecast or a promise of a future price path.

## Operation

- Automatic generation is off by default. An administrator can enable it and choose a Tehran-local time (default 08:05). The scheduler checks each minute; delivery follows data collection and AI completion.
- A manual generation button uses the same daily row, enforced by a database unique constraint per symbol/Tehran calendar date. Known pre-AI data/credential failures can be retried manually; completed, paid or ambiguous attempts cannot. There is no automatic retry after a failed attempt.
- If another analysis holds the shared lock, the scheduled job tries again on subsequent ticks without consuming the daily allowance. A report can therefore arrive later than its configured start time.
- The existing AI connection/model selection is reused. API connections incur additional inference usage; token counts are stored with each report. No real paid request was made during development.
- Running work has a 15-minute total deadline and a 30-minute shared lock lease. Interrupted rows are marked failed after 32 minutes; a restart does not re-run an interrupted day's inference.
- An old completed report remains visible when today's attempt fails, with its own date and an explicit stale warning. The archive exposes the latest 30 attempts for the configured symbol.

## Data and output

Fetch 90 days of Bitunix 4h candles; aggregate only complete UTC days and complete Monday-start weeks. Require contiguous recent data and a fresh quote before inference. Supply 80 daily bars, up to 12 completed weeks and 120 4h bars. Optional source-labelled market intelligence is supplementary. No news or economic-calendar feed is connected.

The validated report includes weekly/daily structure, dominant and alternative scenarios, activation/invalidation conditions, two to four conditional stages, referenced target levels, uncertainty, what to monitor, and changes from the prior completed report. Level IDs must reference deterministic levels from the supplied data. Narrative claims are generated by the model and are not independently proven by schema validation.

The interactive TradingView Lightweight Charts panel uses the report’s actual closed 4h OHLC snapshot (daily fallback for older reports). It overlays dashed dominant/alternative paths through known scenario levels over a seven-day display horizon. Waypoint times are evenly spaced for presentation, not model-predicted arrival dates. No future candles or invented prices are generated. The chart is a report snapshot, not a live feed.

## Upgrade and production compatibility

Startup creates two additive tables (`daily_outlook_settings`, `daily_outlook_runs`) through the existing SQLAlchemy initialization. Back up the database and retain a copy of local production diffs before deploying. No production deployment is performed by this change.

This branch also preserves the three manually applied production fixes reported in the support session: market context reads from `_artifacts`; terminal setups no longer block same-direction proposals; regular hourly analysis aligns to fixed UTC interval boundaries plus one minute and successful manual runs do not move that schedule. The corresponding old completion-relative scheduler assertion is updated.

After deployment, open the new tab as an administrator, choose a time, save, and generate the first report. Verify its timestamps, candle source, scenarios and token usage. Enabling daily generation does not enable hourly automation or live trading.

Backend tests use an isolated test database, offline market data and mocked AI responses. Coverage includes complete-bar aggregation, UTC Monday weeks, stale/gapped data rejection, known-level validation, Tehran scheduling, daily deduplication, lock contention, failure handling, stored usage, admin-only writes and absence of entry-signal writes. Frontend tests cover stale reports, conditional scenarios, schedule controls and generation.

## Validation-failure recovery

Manual retries reuse the same daily row only for known data/credential failures before inference, with no model, usage or request-start marker. Scheduled retries remain disabled. Prior validation errors are retained in `context.pre_ai_failures` (latest 20). A request-start marker is committed before contacting AI; ambiguous timeouts and paid failures remain blocked. The dashboard exposes `can_retry_today` separately from `attempted_today`.

The entire 4h history is still checked for finite positive prices, ordered high/low ranges, continuity and freshness. Body containment is strict for the latest 120 raw bars sent to AI. Earlier constituent body anomalies are tolerated only when the complete daily aggregate is valid and its unchanged high/low contains every anomalous open/close; otherwise generation fails. No prices are clamped or invented. Each tolerated source anomaly is disclosed in the context and dashboard. This validates the representation supplied to AI; it does not certify the exchange's underlying data as correct.


## Daily report timeline and static charts

The outlook landing page is a paginated timeline (30 reports per page), inspired by OptionFlew's reports list. Each completed post opens a dedicated hash URL, `#/outlook/reports/{id}`, supporting reload and browser back. Failed/running attempts retain their date and status. Opening a report never generates a new AI analysis.

Each report page starts with a downloadable PNG, rendered by Matplotlib from that report's stored closed 4h candles (daily fallback for older snapshots). It is a real OHLC chart rendered by the application, not a live TradingView widget or screenshot. The renderer never fetches current data, repairs prices, or synthesizes future candles. Missing/invalid snapshots produce an explicit image error while the report text remains accessible. Authentication is required for both detail and image endpoints.

New AI reports can specify `path_level_ids` (2–6 known IDs, ending at a target). Older reports use activation then target IDs as a schematic fallback. Both scenario lines start at the saved quote/as-of and end seven days later; all waypoint prices come from referenced levels. The image and Persian caption explicitly distinguish conditional scenario paths from known candles, and schematic spacing from predicted arrival times.

GET `/api/gui/daily-outlook/runs?before_id=N` paginates archive metadata. GET `/api/gui/daily-outlook/runs/{id}/chart-image` returns a PNG data URL and download filename. Rendering runs off the event loop, is serialized for Matplotlib safety, and caches at most eight snapshots in process memory. No schema migration or extra provider cost is required.

## Scenario recap and technical overlays

The report ends with a dated recap of the existing dominant thesis, activation, path, targets, invalidation and alternative activation. It does not re-run AI or claim that historical conditions are currently active.

The chart labels up to three candidate supports (S1–S3) below the saved quote and resistances (R1–R3) above it, selected from the saved daily/weekly/range extrema. Levels closer than 0.15% of the quote are deduplicated per side. These labels are relative to the quote, not proof of support/resistance reactions.

A descriptive parallel channel is fitted to confirmed 2-left/2-right high/low pivots in the last 60 closed candles (minimum 40). Both sides require at least two touches spread over one third of the window. Slope disagreement must remain within max(ATR, 35% of channel width) over the window; width must exceed ATR; at least 85% of closes must fit within the bounds plus 0.35 ATR tolerance. A latest close or saved quote outside those bounds suppresses the channel. Gaps, too few pivots and failed fits also suppress it. The daily fallback often has fewer than 40 bars and therefore shows no channel. These thresholds are display heuristics, not backtested trading signals or confidence probabilities.

Blue solid bounds/midline describe history; blue dash-dot extension is geometric extrapolation, separate from the report's conditional scenario paths. The Persian legend explains the channel and lists exact S/R prices and source labels. The snapshot stays fixed; no new AI or exchange requests are made.
